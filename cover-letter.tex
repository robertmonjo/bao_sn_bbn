\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit the manuscript \textbf{``Coasting cosmology compatible with BAO and nucleosynthesis''} by Robert Monjo and Ardra E.\ Sasi for consideration in \textit{Physics Letters B}.

In this work, we confront a recent coasting-universe model with three independent datasets: the DESI
DR1 compressed BAO catalogue (ten galaxy and QSO tracers, $z=0.295$--$1.491$), the Pantheon$+$ 50-bin supernovae compilation, and the two primordial-abundance
observables from big bang nucleosynthesis. A constant-$\alpha$ fit to the DESI
DR1 BAO data yields $\Delta\mathrm{AIC}=0.68$ relative to flat $\Lambda$CDM,
showing that a single geometric parameter is sufficient to match current BAO
precision. A running-$\alpha$ extension with three free parameters reproduces
the observed helium fraction ($Y_p=0.2454\pm0.0034$, within $0.03\sigma$ of the
measured value) and the deuterium-to-hydrogen ratio over the nucleosynthesis
window, with $\Delta\mathrm{AIC}_{\rm tot}=-2.9$ against the two-parameter
$\Lambda$CDM reference. This mild preference is stable over
$H_0\in[65,74]\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$. We also identify an open
constraint: the fitted baryon-to-photon ratio is approximately $27\%$ above the
CMB-inferred value, which motivates a future hyperconical treatment of
recombination.

The results are fully reproducible from the public repository \url{https://github.com/robertmonjo/bao_sn_bbn}.
We believe our manuscript is well suited for \textit{Physics Letters B} given its
focus on compact, testable proposals in fundamental cosmology.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere and is not under consideration at any other journal. All authors have read and approved the submission.

\closing{Sincerely,}
